Today, I would like to walk you through my project: AURA BI, an AI-powered Business Intelligence and Research Consultant Platform. AURA BI is designed to solve a critical problem in corporate strategy: the sheer amount of time it takes to research a company, analyze its competitors, and synthesize actionable AI-driven opportunities. Instead of spending days compiling manual reports, AURA BI automates this entire process. You simply input a company name, and our multi-agent AI swarm crawls the web, researches the profile, and generates a high-fidelity interactive dashboard. Let's see it in action by searching for a company. As the search initiates, let me explain the workflow and technical reasoning behind the scenes. The backend is built with FastAPI and LangGraph, coordinating a 6-agent collaborative AI swarm. We use a sequential state graph. First, the Research Agent gathers core facts and financials. Then, the News Agent finds recent expansions. This context is passed to the Business Analyst Agent, which builds a SWOT analysis, and the Competitor Agent, which benchmarks against peers. Finally, the AI Consultant proposes ROI-driven AI opportunities, and the CEO Pitch Agent formulates an executive memo. A major technical decision I made was to use a multi-agent architecture via LangGraph rather than a single LLM prompt. This allows for specialization, reduces AI hallucinations, and ensures each agent can focus on complex, isolated reasoning tasks effectively. Here is the generated corporate intelligence dashboard. On the frontend, I used React, TypeScript, and Vite, styled with Tailwind CSS to create a modern, glassmorphism UI that feels premium and interactive. Notice the SWOT Quadrants here, providing immediate strategic metrics. Below that, the Benchmarking Table compares the company against top sector peers in terms of revenue, employees, and tech stacks. We also have interactive Recharts displaying cumulative forecasts and expense ratios. My decision to use Recharts and Framer Motion was to ensure the data isn't just static, but highly engaging and easy to digest for executives. Beyond just data aggregation, AURA BI provides actionable intelligence. Here, the AI Consultant Agent has proposed three concrete, ROI-driven AI opportunities tailored specifically to the company's operational bottlenecks, analyzing implementation costs and timelines. And finally, the CEO Memo Box—a formal brief formulated by our Pitch Agent, ready to be sent directly to the C-suite. We built this to bridge the gap between raw data and executive decision-making. The decision to output structured schemas using Pydantic ensures that the LLM responses map perfectly to these UI components without breaking the frontend. For offline sharing, the platform supports one-click exports. It dynamically generates a PDF report or a widescreen PowerPoint deck using ReportLab and python-pptx on the backend. This ensures the research is immediately presentation-ready. Building AURA BI allowed me to integrate my skills in full-stack web development with advanced AI agent orchestration.
